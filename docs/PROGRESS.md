# Implementation progress

## Current status — responsive web-first, multi-school SIMS for private Grade 1–12 schools

The product targets independent private primary and secondary schools as organization tenants. Supported academic levels are Grades 1–12 only; kindergarten, college, university, and higher-education campus management are excluded. Campus-scoped records refer to school operational sites. The system is designed for browser use first, with responsive layouts for phones, tablets, and desktops.

The approved plan and playbook are the scope authority. This pass establishes a runnable bilingual web/API foundation and the first registrar/teacher workflows; it does **not** complete Release 1.

Authorized registrars can record an individually reviewed promotion, repetition, or graduation outcome into the new year; the student profile shows the campus-scoped year-by-year enrolment history. Every decision is reasoned and audited, and destination sections are capacity-checked. No school grading or promotion rules are assumed or applied automatically.

Public intake is available through campus-specific links (see SIS completion workflow); guardian relationship verification and invitation-based portal provisioning are implemented.
- Authentication/account lifecycle extension: SMTP password recovery with a generic request response, one-time hashed 30-minute reset tokens, password confirmation, immediate access/refresh-session revocation using a per-user auth version, per-IP rate limits, and audit events for reset/login/logout/session refresh. Added school-organization-scoped account listing and activation/deactivation; status changes revoke all sessions. The account manager is available to authorized school roles. Accounts remain single-role; MFA and multiple roles are future enhancements.
- Student profile maintenance: campus-scoped updates to student names, gender, date of birth, previous school and guardian contact phone; profile and guardian-link changes are audited without copying contact values into the new audit snapshots. Registrar/principal/director/system-admin roles alone can view or manage guardian links; teachers do not receive guardian contact phones in student list/detail or dashboard responses.
- Student CSV import: UTF-8 upload with template, 600-row/1 MB limits, per-row field and active-section validation, duplicate ID/name-and-birthdate rejection, shared guardian-phone warnings, dry-run preview, and atomic valid-row import. Each imported student receives a first-login password-change account; returned credentials are no-store, downloaded as a dedicated CSV, and retained only in the page state until the operator confirms they were recorded.
- Guardian contacts can be linked/unlinked to students using the existing schema. New links are unverified with results/fees permissions disabled; verification and any portal permissions are explicit and audited. The bilingual profile UI includes primary guardian marking, access controls, invitation support, and unlink confirmation.
- Phase 7 sensitive student data: student ID number, health notes, and learning-support notes are stored using AES-256-GCM with a dedicated `SENSITIVE_DATA_KEY`; student ID lookup uses a derived-key HMAC. Ordinary student APIs omit these values. Explicit reveal and update routes are role- and campus-scoped, no-store, and audit field names without values. Key deployment/backup requirements are documented; consent automation and scheduled retention remain deferred.
- Phase 7 emergency contacts: authorized staff can add, list and remove prioritized emergency contacts from the student profile. Contact names and phone numbers are encrypted at rest; reveal and management paths are campus-scoped, no-store where applicable, and audited without plaintext values.
- Phase 8 timetable: campus periods by morning/afternoon/evening shift, campus rooms, section shifts, weekly section/teacher views, room-aware assignment entry, hard teacher/section/room collision checks, approved-leave-linked date-specific substitutes, and a print-friendly weekly grid. Timetable APIs and views preserve campus and staff visibility; substitutions appear only on their scheduled date.
- Phase 9 grading/report-card workflow: marks can be linked to a campus term and subject; campus administrators configure bilingual grade bands and choose between pooled-points or equal-subject weighting. A school administrator explicitly confirms the scale and method; confirmation is audited and automatically invalidated when the scale changes. Marks progress through audited draft → submitted → approved → locked/published transitions; a different user must approve a mark than its author, and its approver cannot publish it. Authorized campus leadership can amend published marks with a required reason; old values remain in audit history and corrections return to draft for review. Official report revisions snapshot locked marks and move through draft → submitted → approved → published with distinct submitter, approver, and publisher identities. Revising a published report requires a reason; the previous published revision stays immutable and the replacement must pass the complete workflow. Changes to the confirmed grading policy or source marks block transitions of stale snapshots. Verified guardians receive only published report snapshots permitted by the child's guardian-link access.
- Phase 10 fee operations: campus-scoped bilingual fee items, issued invoices with immutable line-description/price snapshots, manual partial/full payments and receipts, audited payment reversals, and reasoned voiding of unpaid invoices. No amounts or fee items are seeded; payment gateways are not connected.
- Phase 11 cashier/expense controls: payments, reversals and expenses must be recorded against the caller's active currency-matched shift; shift closing records counted cash, expected cash and variance; expenses are append-only and audited; a date-ranged campus income-minus-expense summary is grouped by currency.
- Phase 12 communications foundation: leadership can publish audited bilingual campus announcements and add events to an audited campus calendar; authorized campus users can read published announcements and date-overlapping events in the bilingual UI. No external delivery is connected.
- Phase 14 notification slice: campus announcements and events create bilingual in-app notifications for active users authorized to view communications; each recipient can see their own active-campus inbox and mark notices read. The UI includes an unread count and notification panel. SMS/email delivery, preferences, consent and outbox/retries remain deferred.
- Phase 15 welfare/conduct first slice: authorized campus leadership and counsellors can record/read bilingual student commendations in the student profile; each record is campus-scoped and audited. Incident reports, guardian meetings, counselling notes, conduct grades and scheduled automation remain deferred.
- Phase 17 reporting first slice: the attendance workflow includes a date-ranged status summary, section breakdown and attendance rate, with teacher assignment scope and a maximum 367-day range. Attendance rate counts present + late as attended and excludes excused records from the denominator. The loaded attendance and term-result summaries can be exported to bilingual-friendly CSV; wider report configuration and group dashboards remain deferred.
- Phase 17 reporting next slice: marks include a campus- and term-scoped provisional results summary grouped by subject, using points-weighted percentages and distinct student/assessment counts. It includes draft marks, enforces teacher assignment scope, and is exposed in the bilingual marks page. The MySQL implementation aggregates in the database rather than relying on the capped marks-list endpoint.
- SaaS platform first slice: platform administrators can provision schools, suspend/reactivate them, configure subscription plans and module entitlements, assign plans, issue manual invoices and record payments. The 30-day Starter trial is configurable. Authentication and API access enforce school lifecycle/subscription status, module entitlements and student/staff plan caps. School owners can view subscription details, download a versioned organization-scoped JSON export (including uploaded admissions files), and request closure; closure approval requires a completed download and records separate personal-data and financial retention deadlines. After the personal-data deadline, an explicitly confirmed, audited platform action pseudonymizes student/guardian/applicant/staff identifiers, disables tenant accounts, removes uploaded admission documents and sensitive student fields, redacts snapshots/audit before/after payloads, and preserves academic/financial history under pseudonymous IDs.

The migration is applied locally; the first platform administrator has not yet been provisioned. Organization owners can create branches subject to the subscription site limit; all school administrators receive membership in new branches.
- SaaS limitations: personal-data pseudonymization is an explicit manual operator action, not scheduled; financial records are retained past their configured deadline until a separately reviewed purge workflow is implemented. Billing is manual, and school self-service closure remains export-first with platform approval rather than immediate deletion. Password hashes and active session/invitation tokens are excluded from portability exports by design.

Owner branch switches rotate refresh tokens, are audited, and issue branch-scoped sessions; access to a deactivated branch is rejected immediately.
